Cask and casque picture and application icon
============================================

  Cassque.png   (the picture in Cassque's About box)
  Resources/Icons/Cassque.ico   (referenced from Cassque.rc)
  Resources/Icons/Cassque.png   (the 1024-pixel master)

All three are built by Assets/Icon/build_cassque_icon.py. The barrel is drawn
by that script; the casque on it is the top of the cassowary silhouette in
Resources/Icons/Casso-0-silhouette.png, which, like Casso's own icons, is
derived from "Mrs Cassowary closeup" (c) by Mr. Smiley / BunyipCo, the
photograph Casso's README shows.
