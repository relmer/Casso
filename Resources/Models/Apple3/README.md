# Apple3

The Apple /// body, from the front of the upper body to the back, drawn under
the Monitor /// in the 3D desk scene. The sloped keyboard section in front
of it is not modeled.

Generated by `scripts/modelgen/cad_apple3.py`; regenerate there and copy
`Apple3.mesh` and `Apple3.mtl` here. Model space is millimeters, X right, Y
back, Z up, with the bottom of the front face at y = 0.

## Measurements

- **Width, 444.5 mm**, Apple's figure (Apple /// Plus Owner's Guide,
  appendix D).
- **The profile** was read from the side photograph at 0.265 mm per pixel,
  scaled by the whole machine's documented depth of 462.3 mm: the top 141.8
  mm above the desk; the front face from 44.8 mm up at its bottom edge to
  the top, its top edge 17.2 mm back, a lean of about 10 degrees; the
  cover's lower edge sloping from the face down to 27.8 mm at 79.5 mm back,
  then level to the fins at 238.5 mm; the fins' back face at 282.2 mm.
- **The rear** was read from the rear photograph at 0.276 mm per pixel,
  scaled by the casting's width: the fins on a 12.7 mm pitch down to 30.5 mm
  above the desk; the slot openings 172.5 to 269 mm from the left as seen
  from behind and 42.8 to 125.6 mm high; the legs, the connector plate
  (34.5 to 285.7 mm) and the power plate (316 to 407 mm); and the connectors'
  centers, 11.9 mm above the desk.
- **The face** was read from the front photograph at 0.32 mm per pixel. The
  slot is 137.8 mm wide, the width the Disk /// is scaled from, centered at
  313 mm from the left. The drive's latch, recess and lamp follow the Disk
  ///'s, which has the same mechanism.
- **The floppy disk connector** is the 26-position shrouded header modeled
  on the Disk ///.
- **The feet, the inset panel and the side fins' pitch** are by eye.

Casso's silhouette replaces the logo on the first logo plate.
