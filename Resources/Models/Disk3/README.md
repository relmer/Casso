# Disk3

The Apple Disk /// (A3M0004), the Apple ///'s external drive, drawn beside
the Apple /// in the 3D desk scene.

Generated by `scripts/modelgen/cad_disk3.py`; regenerate there and copy
`Disk3.mesh` and `Disk3.mtl` here. Model space is millimeters, X right, Y
back, Z up, with the upper front face at y = 0 facing the viewer.

## Measurements

No Apple document with the Disk ///'s dimensions has been found, so every
dimension comes from the photographs.

- **Width, 159.9 mm.** The slot is assumed to be the Apple ///'s built-in
  slot, 137.8 mm, scaled from the Apple ///'s documented 444.5 mm width in
  `disk3-beside-a3-front-rr3483512.jpg` and TCD `fig03`. The case is 1.163
  times as wide as the slot in `disk3-front-oc.jpg`, by a pixel-run script.
- **Height, 98.9 mm with the feet.** The case and the feet are 1783 pixels
  of the front photograph against 2882 for the width.
- **Depth, 229 mm.** The footprint in `disk3-bottom-oc.jpg` is 2833 by 1975
  pixels by a script that finds the case color in rows and columns, an aspect
  of 1.434.
- **Front features** were read from `disk3-front-oc.jpg` at 0.148 mm per
  pixel of a 1200-pixel copy, good to about 1 mm: the slot at 51.5 to 56 mm
  above the desk, the notch 41.5 mm wide from 22.8 to 83.4 mm, the latch's
  tab from 46.4 to 61.2 mm, the lamp centered at 33.1 mm, the drive label at
  9.3 to 25.1 mm from the left and the logo plate at 12.7 to 45.8 mm.
- **Feet** are 22 mm squares, 25.9 and 113.6 mm from the left and 55.6 and
  179.6 mm from the front, from the bottom photograph at 0.2 mm per pixel.
- **Chamfers, the block's projection, the top panel's inset and the seam's
  height** are by eye from the three-quarter views.
