## README section

### Casso Explorer

What do you get when you cross CiderPress with ADTPro... and a cassowary? **Casso Explorer.**

Manage your Apple II disk images with style and panache. Click into a `.dsk`, `.po`, `.nib` or `.woz` like any other folder on your PC, and there are your files: DOS 3.3 or ProDOS, subdirectories and all. Drag files in, drag them out, rename them, lock them, make new folders, and undo the whole lot with Ctrl+Z. In the preview pane, BASIC programs appear as listings, hi-res and double hi-res pictures appear on a CRT, and everything else opens as text or hex.

Got a real Apple on the desk? Plug it in. With CassoLink, the disks in its drives appear in the tree right beside your folders, over a serial cable, an Uthernet II or a Wi-Fi modem. Copy files or whole disks in either direction. No disk to boot? Have Casso Explorer type the loader into the Apple for you, or send it through the cassette input.

And when you finally dig that shoebox of floppies out of the attic, start a **Full Send**, then just swap disks until you run out. It's completely automatic: each new disk is found the moment it's in a drive and sent, imaged into a folder, numbered, checked for duplicates and listed in a manifest, without even having to press a key between disks.

Found a keeper? Send it straight into a drive in Casso, or open it in a new Casso with one click.
