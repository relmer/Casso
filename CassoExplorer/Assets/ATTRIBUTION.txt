Application icon and About box pictures
======================================

  CassoExplorer.png                   (the icon, in Casso Explorer's About box)
  Cassowary.png                       (the photograph heading the About box)
  Resources/Icons/CassoExplorer.ico   (referenced from CassoExplorer.rc)
  Resources/Icons/CassoExplorer.png   (the 1024-pixel master)

All are built by Assets/Icon/build_casso_explorer_icon.py. The folder follows
Windows' own File Explorer icon in shape and color so the two read as a pair;
the Apple bands down its left edge and the cassowary on its front are Casso's.
Like Casso's own icons, the silhouette is derived from "Mrs Cassowary closeup"
(c) by Mr. Smiley / BunyipCo, the photograph Casso's README shows, and
Cassowary.png is that photograph.
