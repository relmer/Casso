Cask and casque pictures and application icon
=============================================

  CassoExplorer.png        (the picture in CassoExplorer's About box)
  CassoExplorerCask.png    (the bare cask, in the About box's explanation of the name)
  CassoExplorerCasso.png   (Casso's icon, in the same explanation)
  Resources/Icons/CassoExplorer.ico   (referenced from CassoExplorer.rc)
  Resources/Icons/CassoExplorer.png   (the 1024-pixel master)

All are built by Assets/Icon/build_cassque_icon.py. The barrel is drawn by
that script. The casque on it is the top of the cassowary silhouette in
Resources/Icons/Casso-0-silhouette.png, and CassoExplorerCasso.png is that icon
scaled down. Like Casso's own icons, the silhouette is derived from
"Mrs Cassowary closeup" (c) by Mr. Smiley / BunyipCo, the photograph Casso's
README shows.
